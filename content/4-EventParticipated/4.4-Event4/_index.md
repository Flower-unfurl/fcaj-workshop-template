# Reflection on "FCAJ - Agentic AI Build Week"

### Purpose of the Event

- Share practical experience that allows participants to directly build and transform ideas on paper into real technology products that solve real-world problems.
- Emphasize the combination of programming skills and business thinking.
- Inspire the younger generation to break out of old molds, embrace innovation, and take ownership of technologies such as AI.

### Speaker List

- **Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung** - Head of Solution Architect of Vietnam
- **Mr. Joseph Marazota** - Head of Technology of ASEAN
- Representatives from outstanding Hackathon teams presenting their projects: **One Team**, **Signal Scout**, **Plan**, **3K**, and **Six Pillar**.

### Highlights

#### Opening Keynote by Mr. Joseph Marazota
- He emphasized the rapid pace of change in modern technology compared to the era of legacy systems and affirmed the role of the younger generation in shifting mindsets.
- He encouraged young professionals to become "lifelong learners" and stressed that humans will always serve as the "human in the loop" — retaining final decision-making authority when applying AI.

#### Team One Team — AI Chatbot for KFC Ordering
- Built an AI agent that supports multi-channel ordering (Zalo, WhatsApp), allowing customers to place orders without downloading an app or creating an account.
- The system uses AWS Agent Core to retain conversation history, scrapes data from the website, and interacts with customers in a natural, human-like manner — reducing friction in the purchasing process.

#### Team Signal Scout — Business Strategy Analysis
- Focused on the Business Model Canvas, the team built a multi-agent system to aggregate fragmented information about competitors.
- The AI summarizes, scores, and forecasts revenue and risk if the company adopts a competitor's strategy.

#### Team Plan — AI Architect for Solution Architects
- Built an application that analyzes natural language from customer requirements to automatically generate architecture diagrams (on Draw.io), compute pricing estimates, and produce infrastructure-as-code (e.g., Terraform) for automated AWS deployment — all within minutes.

#### Team 3K — Project Shepherd: Crowd Monitoring
- Applied a real-time computer vision system using YOLO and AWS Kinesis to monitor crowding conditions at airports and supermarkets.
- The AI agent monitors, analyzes congested zones in real time, and recommends actionable responses for on-site operations staff.

#### Team Six Pillar — Adaptive Workflow Engine: Anti-Money Laundering
- Provided a banking solution to automate the investigation of suspicious transactions.
- The AI agent cross-checks customer profiles, fund flows, and blacklists, reducing manual investigation time from several hours to just minutes — with a human expert making the final call.

### Lessons Learned

- **Business problem first**: No matter how sophisticated the technology, it cannot overcome the limitations of an ill-defined business problem. Always know clearly what problem the product solves and what pain point the customer has, rather than focusing purely on showcasing technology.
- **Scope control**: In a competition or short-term project, clearly defining what is in-scope and out-of-scope is critical. Build only an MVP with just enough features to run a demo — avoid scope creep that prevents timely delivery.
- **The power of teamwork**: Setting aside personal ego, trusting teammates, and clearly dividing responsibilities (AI, Backend, UI, Presentation) are the keys to delivering an enormous workload under time pressure.
- **A working demo is everything**: A great idea on paper is meaningless without a practical demonstration proving that it actually works.

### Application to Work

- **Problem-solving mindset**: Always ask "Who does this product serve? What problem does it solve?" before writing a single line of code.
- **Optimization with AI Agents**: AI Agent systems can be applied to automate repetitive, time-consuming internal tasks (e.g., data scraping, report aggregation, document analysis, diagram generation, Terraform creation).
- **Integrating "Human-in-the-loop"**: When deploying AI in sensitive domains (such as finance or banking), learn to treat AI as an assistant rather than a decision-maker. All critical final decisions must still be reviewed and approved by a human.
- **Flexible use of Cloud services**: Learn from how the teams combined AWS architectures (Bedrock, Agent Core, Lambda, DynamoDB, Kinesis) to build secure, AI-memory-capable, and rapidly scalable systems.

### Experience at the Event

Attending the **"FCAJ - Agentic AI Build Week"** event was an intensely hands-on and memorable experience, giving me a front-row seat to how teams transform ideas into real technology products under time pressure. Some notable highlights:

#### Learning from Highly Experienced Speakers
- Gained inspiration from senior leaders such as Mr. Joseph Marazota regarding the technology landscape over the next 2–3 years, as well as the importance of dismantling legacy mindsets to embrace bold innovation and creativity.

#### Hands-on Technical Experience
- Directly observed live demos from each competing team:
  - **One Team**: Watched the automated AI ordering flow in real time via Zalo, along with a dashboard displaying customer chat history.
  - **Signal Scout**: Observed the AI generate a competitor analytics dashboard with business strategy risk forecasting.
  - **Plan**: Experienced the AI instantly generate architecture diagrams and infrastructure code (Terraform) directly from natural language input.
  - **3K**: Watched the real-time people-counting camera system identify congestion zones and suggest resolution actions for staff.
  - **Six Pillar**: Observed the screen where AI automatically analyzed evidence of suspicious transactions and suggested handling actions (dismiss, request more information, or escalate) for financial compliance officers.

#### Application of Modern Tools
- Got direct exposure to the integration of cutting-edge technologies, including: AWS Bedrock, LLMs from OpenAI/Anthropic, YOLOv8 for Computer Vision, Terraform for infrastructure-as-code generation, and Agent Core with long-term memory capabilities.

#### Networking and Exchange
- The event fostered a vibrant and energetic team-working atmosphere.
- Had the opportunity to engage and converse directly with highly experienced speakers and fellow participants.

#### Takeaways
- **Step outside your comfort zone**: Don't be afraid of lacking skills — participate boldly to gain real-world experience. Prioritize learning and building connections over pressuring yourself to win.
- **Lower your ego, elevate your team**: In high-pressure environments, set aside personal ego and trust one another. The team must divide responsibilities clearly from the very beginning to avoid wasting precious time.
- **Control project scope and stay practical**: Manage project boundaries strictly and avoid scope creep at all costs to stay on schedule. No matter how brilliant the idea, it is meaningless without a working demo that proves the product actually functions.
